EN: **The negative result that remains.** Along the path from "tense" to "calmer" arousal falls, so brightness should fall; but valence rises, and the bells' register rises with it. The two cues push the spectral centroid in opposite directions, and in the mix register wins (ρ = −0.49). The drone faithfully follows brightness (ρ = 0.97), but its centroid varies little (from 309 to 319 Hz). It is a cue conflict consistent with the additivity found by Eerola et al. (2013): additive cues add up even when their signs are opposite. It is neither hidden nor "fixed" by decree, because it is unclear which choice people perceive as calmer: linking register to arousal too, or letting valence brighten the sound. It is a question for study V-7.

## 12.5 | Struttura stocastica e sicurezza | Stochastic structure and safety

IT: I test di unità verificano le proprietà del modello senza audio. UT-SND-02 percorre una griglia di 121 stati più valori fuori scala o non numerici, a tre ore diverse, e controlla che nessuno superi i limiti della Tabella 9.3. UT-SND-03 verifica la direzione di ogni mappatura (per esempio che densità, brillantezza e livello crescano con l'arousal e che il registro cresca con la valenza). UT-SND-05 verifica che la partitura parta dallo stato dichiarato, finisca sul bersaglio, attraversi le tre fasi in ordine, cambi modo solo al confine di frase e non salti più di 0,03 nel piano tra due punti di controllo. UT-SND-06 verifica il processo di Poisson su due ore: numero di eventi entro quattro deviazioni standard dal valore atteso e coefficiente di variazione degli intervalli tra 0,8 e 1,2. UT-SND-07 verifica che ogni altezza appartenga al modo e che il moto sia prevalentemente congiunto.
EN: Unit tests check the model's properties without audio. UT-SND-02 sweeps a grid of 121 states plus out-of-range or non-numeric values, at three hours, and checks that none exceeds the limits of Table 9.3. UT-SND-03 checks the direction of each mapping (for example that density, brightness and level grow with arousal and register with valence). UT-SND-05 checks that the score starts on the declared state, ends on the target, goes through the three phases in order, changes mode only on phrase boundaries and never jumps more than 0.03 in the plane between two control points. UT-SND-06 checks the Poisson process over two hours: event count within four standard deviations of the expectation and inter-event coefficient of variation between 0.8 and 1.2. UT-SND-07 checks that every pitch belongs to the mode and that motion is mainly stepwise.

IT: **V-7**, nuovo nella 6.0, riguarda il paesaggio sonoro. Disegno entro i soggetti, adulti volontari, tre condizioni di 10 minuti in ordine bilanciato: paesaggio con traiettoria ISO; paesaggio fermo sul bersaglio; silenzio. Misure prima e dopo con il Self-Assessment Manikin (valenza, arousal) e il gradimento. Con un effetto atteso medio (d = 0,45), α = 0,05 e potenza 0,80 in un confronto appaiato servono circa 40 persone. EN: **V-7**, new in 6.0, concerns the soundscape. Within-subject design, adult volunteers, three 10-minute conditions in counterbalanced order: soundscape with the ISO trajectory; soundscape held on the target; silence. Measures before and after with the Self-Assessment Manikin (valence, arousal) and liking. With an expected medium effect (d = 0.45), α = 0.05 and power 0.80 in a paired comparison, about 40 people are needed. Analysis with a mixed model (condition and order as fixed effects, person as random effect). Secondary question: which resolution of the A4 conflict is perceived as calmer. The study requires ethics-committee approval and informed consent; it does not involve patients.
